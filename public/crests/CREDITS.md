# Club crest credits

The SVG crests in this folder were downloaded from the open-source
[FCLOGO/fclogo.top](https://github.com/FCLOGO/fclogo.top) collection (MIT-licensed repo,
"no account and unlimited downloads for free"). Club badges are trademarks of their
respective football clubs; this project is a private, non-commercial simulator for personal/
office use and doesn't claim any ownership over them.

Clubs not covered by that collection (mostly lower divisions, plus the Uruguayan, Dutch,
Colombian and Chilean clubs, whose federations aren't in the repo) fall back to a generated
shield instead — see `components/ClubCrest.tsx`.
